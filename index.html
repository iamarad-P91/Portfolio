About Us

A neighborhood coffee house, done properly.

Brew Haven started as a single roaster in a small back room. Today, we still roast in-house every week, in small batches, so every cup we pour is close to the bean it came from. No shortcuts, no syrups pretending to be flavor — just coffee, made with intention.
